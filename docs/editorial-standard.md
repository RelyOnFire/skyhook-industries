# Skyhook Industries editorial standard

The public site should be ambitious without collapsing different levels of evidence into one another.

## Claim classes

1. **Flight heritage** — measured or demonstrated in space.
2. **Prior research** — modeled or analyzed by credible aerospace researchers or institutions.
3. **Skyhook design direction** — a present architectural choice under investigation.
4. **Open target** — a desired value or feature that still depends on modeling, testing, material properties or system trades.

## Rules

- Never use a design target as though it were a validated specification.
- Prefer primary research and mission documentation.
- State conservation-of-momentum and energy consequences explicitly.
- Do not describe electrodynamic reboost as creating energy or as making the complete system fuel-free.
- Do not imply that a transportation-scale tether is feasible with heritage polymer fibers unless a structural model supports the claim.
- Separate the Skyhook architecture from HASTOL, cislunar tether and other prior architectures.
- Use dates only when a funded, measurable program justifies them.
- Archive superseded concepts instead of leaving them mixed into current plans.
