# Original-site continuity

Reviewed 24 September 2026 against the live original at
`https://skyhook-industries.com` and the `dribbble-visual-redesign` branch.

The older site has less current engineering detail, but more human and historical
presence. Its large sky opening, founder portrait, prominent Hoyt–Uphoff paper,
and Comet 26P proposal made the project feel like it had a past and a person
behind it. The redesign's current calculations, research boundaries, Tether Lab,
and charcoal/copper identity remain the baseline.

This pass carries forward the founder portrait from the original `/team` page,
the original ethos line about testing, a clear path to the cislunar paper, and a
more complete account of the early comet proposal. `public/founder-isaac-mattoo.jpg`
is a local copy of the portrait already published on the owner's original site;
it is not third-party stock imagery. The homepage echoes the old sky's depth with
CSS rather than copying a background image whose provenance is unclear.

The original site's exact 4,252 km tether, Kevlar recipe, grabber dimensions,
unqualified cost and safety claims, and 2024–2042 schedule remain historical
claims only. The comet sequence is identified as an archived proposal, not an
active mission. The current system, research, and reference-architecture pages
continue to own present-day technical statements.

The original domain is still live. Retiring it or moving the new site onto that
domain is a separate decision; this content pass does not alter either site's
hosting or the campaign save origins.
